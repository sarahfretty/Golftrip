## Open decision — Catherine Bailey has no handicap

45.0 is within WHS (the ceiling is 54.0) and the formula handles it without a special case.
The problem is competitive, not mathematical: on her Carya card 45 produces **44 points**,
nine clear of the next best score, and she would lead the Cup by a distance she can't lose.

Three ways to settle it, in order of how well they hold up at dinner:

1. **Provisional 45, prizes decided on the last two rounds.** She plays a real card at The
   National that everyone can see, and her index is set from it for Carya and Montgomerie.
   Honest, and the adjustment is public.
2. **Start lower — 30 to 34.** A first-timer off 30 gives 33/32/33 shots, which puts her in
   the same conversation as Jo C (40/38/39) rather than out on her own.
3. **Play for the fun of it, outside the Cup.** She gets a card, dots, points and a place on
   the board, but not the trophy. Least awkward on the night, least fun for her.

Recommendation: option 1, decided and announced before The National, not after it.

Everything else is settled: three courses, ratings and slopes for both tees, stroke indexes
validated, 95% allowance, thirteen final playing handicaps.
